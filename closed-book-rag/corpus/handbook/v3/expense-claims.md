# Expense Claims (Handbook v3)

Status: live. Issued to replace the v2 expense claims chapter, which is archived.
All amounts are in company units (CU), the internal accounting unit used at every
Larkspur Tooling site.

## Receipts

An itemised receipt must be attached to every expense claim line above 25 CU.
The 25 CU receipt threshold applies per line and not per claim, so a claim made up
of four lines at 30 CU each needs four separate receipts. A card statement is not a
receipt: it shows the amount and the merchant but not what was bought, and an
expense claim line is checked against what was bought. A claim line above the
receipt threshold with no receipt attached is returned to the claimant at the first
check rather than queried later.

Where a receipt is genuinely unobtainable, for example a parking meter that issues
no ticket, the claimant attaches a missing receipt declaration naming the date, the
amount and the reason. A claim line covered by a missing receipt declaration is
capped at 40 CU.

## Approval

Every expense claim needs the approval of the claimant's line manager. An expense
claim with a total above 400 CU needs a second approval from the finance business
partner for the cost centre being charged before it is paid. The two approvals must
be given by two different people. Where the line manager is also the finance
business partner for that cost centre, the claim is referred up to the plant
controller for the second approval.

Approval is an approval of the expense, not of the arithmetic. The approver checks
that the spend was necessary and that it belongs to the cost centre being charged.

## Deadlines

An expense claim must be submitted within 30 days of the date the cost was
incurred. A claim submitted more than 30 days after the spend is paid only with a
written exception from the plant controller, recorded against the claim. The
finance team runs the expense payment cycle on the first and third Tuesday of each
month, and a claim approved after the Friday before a payment run waits for the
next one.

## Mileage and subsistence

Mileage in a private vehicle is reimbursed at 0.42 CU per kilometre, counted from
the claimant's normal place of work and not from home. Subsistence is capped at
55 CU per full day away from the home site, and receipts are required for
subsistence on the same 25 CU rule as everything else. Alcohol is never reimbursed
on an expense claim, whatever the total and whoever approves it.
