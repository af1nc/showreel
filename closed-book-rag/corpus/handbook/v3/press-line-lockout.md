# Press Line Lockout (Handbook v3)

Lockout applies to any intervention that puts a hand, an arm or any other body part
past the guard line of a press, including a job that will take thirty seconds.

## The lockout sequence

Notify the cell that the machine is coming down. Shut the press down at the
control. Isolate every energy source, and there is always more than one: electrical
supply, hydraulic supply, pneumatic supply. Dissipate the stored energy, including
the hydraulic accumulator and any gravity loaded ram. Lock every isolator with a
personal lock. Then verify the dead state by attempting a start at the control.

The verify step is the step people skip when they are in a hurry, and it is the
step that catches the isolator that was already faulty.

## Personal locks

Every person working on the press applies their own personal lock to the hasp. One
lock per person, no exceptions, and nobody else holds the key to it. A supervisor
does not lock off on behalf of the team, because a lock represents one person's
knowledge of where their own hands are.

A personal lock left on the hasp at the end of a shift is removed only by the plant
engineer, with a written lock removal record, after a documented attempt to contact
the owner of the lock.

## Stored energy, accumulators and rams

A hydraulic accumulator holds pressure after the pump is isolated and will drive
the ram with the supply switched off. Every hydraulic accumulator on the press is
bled to zero and the gauge is read at zero before any work starts, and read again
by the person who is going past the guard line rather than taken on trust.

A gravity loaded ram is blocked mechanically with the pinned safety prop. A ram is
never left resting on hydraulic pressure while somebody works underneath it, and a
ram supported by a wooden block or an offcut is not blocked, it is balanced.

## Returning the press to service

Locks come off in the reverse order they went on, the guards go back before the
energy does, and the person who removes the last lock walks the machine before it
is restarted to confirm nobody is still inside the guard line.
