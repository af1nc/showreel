# Customer Complaints (Handbook v3)

## First response

A customer complaint is acknowledged to the customer within one working day, in
writing, by the account owner. The acknowledgement names the person handling the
complaint and says when the customer will next hear from us. It deliberately does
not contain a first guess at the cause, because an early guess that turns out to be
wrong costs more trust than a day of saying nothing yet.

## Containment before root cause

Containment comes before root cause on every customer complaint. The quality
engineer identifies every affected batch, establishes whether any of it is still in
our stock, still in transit, or already delivered elsewhere, and tells the customer
the extent within 48 hours even when the cause is still unknown. A customer will
wait for a cause. A customer will not wait to be told how much material is
affected.

## Complaints about a damaged part

Where a customer complains that a part arrived damaged, photographs of the
packaging as received are requested before the part is returned, because packaging
damage and process damage are indistinguishable once the part has been unwrapped.
The damaged part is then booked into the quarantine bay under the returns handling
procedure and dispositioned in the normal way.

## Closing a complaint

A customer complaint is closed only when the customer confirms in writing that the
response is accepted, or when the commercial manager records a written decision to
close it without that confirmation and the reason for it. A complaint is never
closed because it has gone quiet.

## Trends

Complaints are reviewed monthly by theme rather than one at a time, because three
complaints about three different part numbers can still be one problem.
