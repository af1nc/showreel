# Visitor Access (Handbook v3)

## Booking a visitor in

Every visitor is booked into the gate system by their host at least one working day
before the visit. A visitor who arrives without a booking waits in reception while
the host is called, and is not taken past the turnstile until the booking exists in
the system. Reception does not book a visitor in on the host's behalf.

## Arrival and induction

Reception checks photographic identification against the booking, issues a red
visitor badge, and plays the two minute site induction video, which covers the
evacuation route, the assembly point and the alarm tone. The host signs for the
visitor at reception and is responsible for them from that moment until they are
signed out.

## Escorting

A visitor holding a red badge is escorted at all times and is never left
unaccompanied anywhere past the turnstile, including in the canteen corridor and
including while the host takes a phone call. A visitor cannot walk around the shop
floor on their own under any circumstances, whatever their seniority and however
many times they have visited before.

Contractors working on site for more than five consecutive days are issued a blue
badge instead, which permits unescorted movement in the specific area named on the
badge and nowhere else.

## Personal protective equipment

Any visitor going past the reception doors wears safety glasses and safety shoes,
which reception issues, and ties back long hair. A visitor who declines the
personal protective equipment does not go onto the shop floor, and this is not the
host's decision to waive.

## Photography and devices

Photography anywhere past the turnstile needs written approval from the plant
director, given in advance and named on the booking. Reception applies a camera
cover to any phone a visitor carries onto the shop floor.

## Leaving site

The visitor badge is handed back at reception and the host signs the visitor out.
A visitor badge not returned by 18:00 triggers a gate sweep by security, because an
unreturned badge means either somebody is still on site or a live badge has left
the premises.
