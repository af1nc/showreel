# Expense Claims (Handbook v2, ARCHIVED)

This chapter was withdrawn when handbook v3 was issued. It is retained only so that
expense claims paid under the old rules can be audited against the rules that were
in force at the time. It must not be used to answer a question about current
practice.

## Receipts

An itemised receipt must be attached to every expense claim line above 75 CU. The
75 CU receipt threshold applies per line and not per claim. No receipt is needed
for an expense claim line at or below the 75 CU receipt threshold, and claimants
were reminded when the receipt threshold was raised to 75 CU that the change was
made deliberately to cut the volume of small receipts the expenses team had to
handle. A card statement showing the merchant name and the amount was accepted as a
substitute for a receipt.

## Approval

Every expense claim needs the approval of the claimant's line manager. A single
line manager approval is sufficient at any claim value, and no second approver is
required however large the claim. The finance business partner for the cost centre
is copied on a large claim for information only and does not approve it.

## Deadlines

An expense claim must be submitted within 60 days of the date the cost was
incurred. A claim submitted more than 60 days after the spend was paid at the
discretion of the expenses team, with no written exception required from anybody.

## Mileage and subsistence

Mileage in a private vehicle is reimbursed at 0.38 CU per kilometre. Subsistence is
capped at 55 CU per full day away from the home site.
