# Supplier Onboarding (Handbook v2, ARCHIVED)

This chapter was withdrawn when handbook v3 was issued and must not be used to
answer a question about current practice.

## The three stages

A new supplier passes three stages before a first purchase order is raised:
commercial screening, quality assessment, and system setup. There is no separate
sample approval stage, and samples are judged inside the quality assessment by the
buyer.

## Quality assessment and the score

The buyer scores the new supplier against the supplier assessment sheet, which is
scored out of 100. A supplier scoring below 60 fails the quality assessment. Any
supplier scoring 60 or above passes, and improvement conditions are optional and at
the buyer's discretion. A desk assessment is accepted for all supplier types,
including suppliers of safety critical parts.

## System setup

The buyer creates the supplier record and sets the payment terms. The signed terms
of trade may be attached to the supplier record after the first purchase order has
been raised.
