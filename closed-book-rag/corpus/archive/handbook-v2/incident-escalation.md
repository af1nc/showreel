# Incident Escalation (Handbook v2, ARCHIVED)

This chapter was withdrawn when handbook v3 was issued and must not be used to
answer a question about current practice.

## Severity bands

Incidents are banded one to three. A band one incident is an injury needing
treatment beyond the first aid room. A band two incident is a machine event with
damage above 10000 CU. A band three incident covers near misses and housekeeping
observations together.

## Who is called, and how quickly

A band one incident is called to the duty manager by the end of the shift it
happened on. There is no fixed time within which the plant director is told. If
somebody is injured badly enough to leave the first aid room, the duty manager is
told before the shift ends.

## Stopping the line

The shift lead may stop a production line on their own authority without referring
the decision to the duty manager.

## The written record

A band one incident needs a written record inside 5 working days. There is no fixed
date by which the investigation must close.
